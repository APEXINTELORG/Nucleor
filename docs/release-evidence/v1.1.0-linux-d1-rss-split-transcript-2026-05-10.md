## Step 5 — D1 RSS split (the implementation work)

Changes (this commit):
- `tools/run_capped.sh` — `sample_tree` now returns four numbers
  (tree_total_kb, compiler_kb, root_kb, count). `compiler_kb` sums VmRSS
  across descendants whose `/proc/<pid>/comm` matches the launched root
  pid's comm (kernel truncated to TASK_COMM_LEN-1 = 15 chars, derived
  deterministically from `basename(argv[0])` to avoid the setsid pre-exec
  race). The RSS-CAP summary line now emits `peak=N MB compiler=N MB
  root=N MB`, matching the schema produced by
  `tools/rss_estop_lib.ps1` on Windows.
- `tools/check_perf_regression.sh` — parses the new fields, tracks
  cold/hot compiler peaks, enforces
  `cold_max_allowed_compiler_memory_mb` and
  `hot_max_allowed_compiler_memory_mb` when present. Backward-compat:
  baselines without those fields warn but don't fail. `--doctor` now
  includes a `compiler-rss-ceilings` check that FAILs if the two
  ceilings are missing. `json_number` moved above `run_doctor` so the
  doctor can read JSON.
- `tools/perf_baseline_linux.json` — adds
  `cold_compiler_peak_memory_mb`, `hot_compiler_peak_memory_mb`,
  `cold_max_allowed_compiler_memory_mb`,
  `hot_max_allowed_compiler_memory_mb`. Placeholder values mirror the
  process-tree numbers (safe over-approximation; compiler-only RSS
  <= process-tree RSS by construction). `_d1_split_note` flags them
  for tightening at Step 7 on a pinned runner.

## Real perf measurement — NOT for baseline lock

```
$ bash tools/check_perf_regression.sh --cold-samples 2 --hot-samples 2 --verbose
sample cold 1: 5.891s, tree=341MB compiler=339MB root=4MB, cache: miss -> stored (sha=220b50b13b70, size 13 MB)
sample cold 2: 6.828s, tree=341MB compiler=339MB root=4MB, cache: miss -> stored (sha=220b50b13b70, size 13 MB)
sample hot  1: 0.308s, tree=10MB  compiler=8MB   root=3MB, cache: hit (sha=220b50b13b70, size 13 MB)
sample hot  2: 0.324s, tree=5MB   compiler=3MB   root=3MB, cache: hit (sha=220b50b13b70, size 13 MB)
OK POSIX perf: cold=6.36s (max 35.0s) | hot=0.32s (max 0.5s)
            | mem cold_tree=341/350MB hot_tree=10/64MB cold_compiler=339/350MB hot_compiler=8/64MB
note: POSIX gate enforces process-tree + compiler-only RSS (D1 split,
      mirrors tools/perf_baseline.json).
```

Observations:
- Cold compiler-only RSS (339 MB) is 2 MB under process-tree (341 MB) —
  Nucleor's own allocation dominates; clang/lld children add ~2 MB.
- Hot compiler-only RSS (3-8 MB) is significantly under process-tree
  (5-10 MB), so the split clearly separates cache-replay overhead from
  spawned-child overhead.
- Cold wall (6.36 s median) sits comfortably under the v1.0.1-relaxed
  35 s ceiling and is much closer to the v0.8.323 9.05 s baseline than
  the v1.0.1 28 s number. The v1.0.2 cold-path perf-debt sprint may be
  smaller than the v1.0.1 audit-pass-1 note implied; a pinned-runner
  re-lock at Step 7 should validate.

These numbers are NOT locked into the baseline. Step 7 (median-of-medians
on a pinned runner) is the right path to update
`tools/perf_baseline_linux.json` with measured compiler-only ceilings.
